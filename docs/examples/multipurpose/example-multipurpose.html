(04) Inside the studio

How an idea becomes a world.

Three movements, one continuous conversation. No black boxes, no reveal theatre. You see the work as it is being made.

We find the tension worth building around.

Every brand is balancing two true things that pull against each other. We interview the people who know, read the room, and name the tension before a single pixel moves.

  • Stakeholder interviews and category audit
  • Positioning, narrative and naming
  • A one-page creative brief everyone signs

We give it a body you can feel.

Type, colour, image, motion and interface are one material. We prototype in the real medium early, so the identity looks like itself on a screen, a shelf and a stage.

  • Identity system and art direction
  • Product and website design
  • Motion language and launch film

We make it survive contact with the world.

The work is not finished when it looks good in a case study. We document the system, train the team and stay close through the first real launches.

  • Guidelines your team will actually use
  • Build support and design QA
  • A six-week post-launch window
