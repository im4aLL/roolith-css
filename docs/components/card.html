Card

Card is a bordered surface with a radius that groups one unit of content. Every part is optional: header, body, footer, image, title, subtitle, text, and an information row, composed in the order the content needs.

class: .card - defined in modules/_card.scss

Basic usage

The body slot carries the 25px padding. The container adds the surface, the radius, a 1px border, and a 20px bottom margin, so cards stacked in a column keep their own spacing.

Deployment

Build 1284 passed on the staging runner and is ready to promote.

HTML 
<div class="card">
    <div class="card-body">
        <h5 class="card-title">Deployment</h5>
        <p class="card-text">
            Build 1284 passed on the staging runner and is ready to promote.
        </p>
    </div>
</div>

Title, subtitle, text, and information

The text slots are sized for a card, not for page layout. .card-title only adds 5px of bottom padding and keeps the heading scale of its element, so use a real heading or a typography class such as .h5 to set the level.

.card-subtitle is dimmed with 10px of bottom padding, .card-text runs at 14px over 22px lines with 20px below, and .card-information is a block of 80% dimmed text with 20px above it for the closing meta line.

Quarterly report
Revenue and retention

Numbers are final for the quarter ending 30 June, including the adjusted retention figure.

Updated 12 July 2025

HTML 
<div class="card">
    <div class="card-body">
        <h5 class="card-title">Quarterly report</h5>
        <h6 class="card-subtitle">Revenue and retention</h6>
        <p class="card-text">
            Numbers are final for the quarter ending 30 June, including the
            adjusted retention figure.
        </p>
        <p class="card-information">Updated 12 July 2025</p>
    </div>
</div>

Header and footer

Header and footer sit outside the body with 15px by 25px of padding and a 1px border against the body. Both use the 14px muted slot color, which makes them read as supporting chrome around the main content.

Images

.card-img goes directly under the card and runs edge to edge: the card clips its overflow, so the image takes the top corner radius. .card-body-img sits inside the body instead and keeps a 10px margin above and below.

Edge to edge image

Use it for covers, thumbnails, and media summaries.

Image in the body

The image follows the title and keeps the body padding around it.

HTML 
<div class="card">
    <img
        class="card-img"
        src="../images/photo-city-aerial.jpg"
        alt="Placeholder cover image" />
    <div class="card-body">
        <h5 class="card-title">Edge to edge image</h5>
        <p class="card-text">Use it for covers, thumbnails, and media summaries.</p>
    </div>
</div>

<div class="card">
    <div class="card-body">
        <h5 class="card-title">Image in the body</h5>
        <img
            class="card-body-img"
            src="../images/photo-open-book.jpg"
            alt="Placeholder inline image" />
        <p class="card-text">
            The image follows the title and keeps the body padding around it.
        </p>
    </div>
</div>

Colors

A color class fills the card with a palette value and switches the text to the matching on-color. Subtitle and information drop to 70% opacity, and the header and footer borders lighten the fill so they stay visible.

Every palette name except white is generated, including dark and black. The fill comes from the palette rather than the surface token, so a variant holds its color instead of following the page surface; a few palette values are lightened in the dark theme.

Links

The card module defines no anchor styles. Put the action inside the body, usually a button or a text link, and let the card stay a container. The information row is designed to follow that action.

Making the whole card a link also works, but you have to add the missing pieces: the card sets no display, so the anchor keeps its inline box, and there is no hover or focus treatment beyond the global focus ring. Add a block display, a hover state, and a visible focus style, and never nest another link or button inside it.

Accessibility

A card is a container. The heading inside it gives the card its name, so the heading level has to match the surrounding page structure.

  • Use a real heading element for .card-title and keep the levels in order across the page.
  • Give .card-img and .card-body-img an alt attribute. Use an empty value when the image is decorative.
  • When the whole card becomes a link, give it one clear name, usually the title text, and keep the hit area free of nested controls.
  • Do not convey state through the card color alone; keep the label text explicit.

Class reference

Slot classes go inside the card, size comes from your layout, and color classes go on the card element.

Class Applies Notes
.card Base Surface fill, radius, 1px border, clipped overflow, and a 20px bottom margin.
.card-header Slot 14px muted chrome with a bottom border.
.card-body Slot 25px of padding around the content.
.card-footer Slot 14px muted chrome with a top border.
.card-title Text 5px of bottom padding only. The element keeps its heading scale.
.card-subtitle Text Dimmed with 10px of bottom padding.
.card-text Text 14px over 22px lines with 20px below.
.card-information Text Block level, 80% size, dimmed, with 20px above.
.card-img Image Full width of the card, clipped by the radius.
.card-body-img Image Full width inside the body with a 10px vertical margin.
.card-primary Color Any palette name except white: secondary, tertiary, info, success, warning, danger, link, dark, black, light.
